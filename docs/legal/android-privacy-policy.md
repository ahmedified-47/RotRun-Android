## Introduction

RotRun is a digital-wellbeing app for Android. It helps you limit distracting apps with Usage Access and Accessibility, run Focus sessions, and score “Brain Health.”

This policy describes the information RotRun processes on your device and, when you sign in, on your RotRun account. RotRun is provided by an independent developer. Signing in with Google creates a RotRun account. Block lists, rules, streak, Daily XP, and screen-time readings listed below can sync to that account.

If something is not listed here, we do not collect it.

## Information we process

On your device, RotRun stores settings and activity needed to run the app, including:

- Apps you choose to block or exclude, Focus and Brick lists, and shield state. When you are signed in, those lists and rules sync to your account.
- Usage Access totals and app names, used for stats, Brain Health, XP, and widgets. When you are signed in, today’s minutes, pickups, top apps, the category Android already assigns to those apps, hourly totals, and daily totals can sync to your account.
- Focus session state, streak, Brain Health difficulty, theme, app icon, and intervention-style preferences. Streak syncs to your account when you are signed in.
- Brain state such as daily XP, Ask-my-brain counts, and related scores. Daily XP (display name and XP) syncs when you are signed in.
- Whether you have an active RotRun Premium entitlement (product identifier and renewal date from Google Play Billing), stored locally.
- If you sign in, the Google account identifier and email Google shares for sign-in. RotRun does not receive your Google password.
- Messages you send in Talk to Brain, recent turns, and the client context sent with a chat: local time, timezone, XP, streak, screen-time goal, Brain Health, today’s screen time, pickups, focus time, and today’s top apps. You can ask Rot to forget saved memories. Streak and XP stay.
- Microphone audio if you start a voice call. Voice is a Premium feature.

Signing in with Google creates your RotRun account. RotRun does not ask you to register a separate email or password with us.

## Usage Access and blocking

With your permission, RotRun uses Android Usage Access to show usage, and Accessibility to apply shields to apps you select and refresh blocking. If Accessibility is off, RotRun can use display over other apps as a fallback shield. You can also allow RotRun to ignore battery optimisation so blocking keeps running.

Android may ask you to turn these on in system Settings. RotRun does not receive biometric templates.

Usage figures and the names of apps you use or try to open are processed on your device and in RotRun’s widget. When you are signed in, the daily figures described above can sync to your account. We do not operate a website or server that receives a public dashboard of your screen time.

## Health and fitness

RotRun on Android does not read Health Connect, count sit-ups, log runs, or write workouts.

Brain Health is a gamified score based on screen time and Focus. It is not a medical or mental-health diagnosis.

RotRun does not request GPS or precise location permission.

## Camera

If you grant camera access, RotRun uses the camera for Mirror Mode interventions that show a live front-camera preview.

Camera frames are processed on the device for that feature. RotRun does not record, save, or upload photos or video from the camera.

## Notifications and widgets

RotRun can schedule local notifications (for example Ask-my-brain, and an upgrade reminder if you turn that on). These are delivered by Android on your device. RotRun does not use remote push notifications.

A notification may include the display name of an app you were trying to open, so the intervention can continue.

If you add RotRun widgets, they show local data such as Brain Health, streak, and Focus state.

## Leaderboard and Rot chat

The Daily XP leaderboard, when you are signed in, reads display name and XP for the current day. This build does not show a Friends leaderboard and does not use Game Center. RotRun does not share receipts, ransom notes, audio slaps, or duels from the leaderboard.

Talk to Brain asks you to sign in with Google. Chat messages, recent turns, and the client context above are sent so Rot can reply. Forgetting clears those memories. Streak and XP stay.

If you start a voice call, audio is processed by the voice service reached through RotRun’s account service. The key for that service stays on the server.

## Purchases

RotRun Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment. RotRun receives entitlement information such as product identifier, whether the subscription is active, and renewal date. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Google’s privacy policy applies to Play billing.

## How we use information

We use the information above to:

- Provide blocking, Focus, interventions, stats, widgets, and Rot chat you request.
- Remember your settings and streak rewards.
- Keep your account, block lists, streak, Daily XP, and screen-time readings in sync when you are signed in.
- Process Premium purchases through Google Play.
- Respond if you email us.

We do not use your Usage Access or camera data to serve third-party advertising. We do not currently include third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs in RotRun.

## Sharing

RotRun does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by Google when you use Google sign-in or Google Play Billing, and by RotRun’s account service for the account, sync, and chat described above.

If you send Help, Feedback, a bug report, or Contact mail, that message (including any diagnostics you leave in the template, such as app version, Android version, and device model) goes to rotrunsupport@gmail.com so we can reply. We do not share those emails with advertisers.

## Retention

On-device RotRun data remains until you change settings, delete the app, or reset the device. Uninstalling RotRun removes the app’s local store. Data in your RotRun account remains until you ask us to delete it. Purchase records remain with Google under Google’s policies.

Emails you send to rotrunsupport@gmail.com are kept as needed to handle your request and for ordinary correspondence.

## Your choices and rights

You can:

- Turn off Usage Access, Accessibility, display over other apps, Camera, Microphone, or Notifications for RotRun in Android Settings.
- Email rotrunsupport@gmail.com to ask us to delete account data we hold. Deleting the app removes the Google session stored on the device.
- Forget everything Rot remembers. Streak and XP stay.
- Cancel or manage RotRun Premium in Manage Subscription in the app, or in Google Play’s subscription settings.
- Delete the app to remove RotRun’s local data.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold (for example emails you sent us, and account data we hold). Contact rotrunsupport@gmail.com. We will not be able to retrieve Usage Access samples that exist only on your device; use Android Settings for those.

## Children

RotRun is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Usage Access and Accessibility are for the person who turns them on for this device. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email rotrunsupport@gmail.com and we will delete it from our inbox where reasonably possible.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when RotRun’s practices change. Until a revised page is approved, the app links to the current hosted policy. The “Last updated” date at the top of that page will change when a revision is published. Continued use after an update means the revised policy applies to that use.
